# Legacy Walksnail HD Pro research profile

Image building is withheld. The earlier HDPro_1 tester payload contains CPU
instructions incompatible with the target Cortex-A7. Subsequent investigation
also established a VI geometry rejection in a corrected research candidate.
The earlier physical boot attempts failed; they are not evidence of a working
camera port. HD Pro I and HD Pro II remain distinct camera targets.

The Firmware panel can inspect a donor and explain the blocker. Both application
build APIs and the standalone image builder refuse image creation. Retained
research sources and the old patch are historical inputs, not an admitted image.
Do not flash an earlier HD Pro tester image as a working camera adaptation.

A replacement requires corrected CPU/startup and geometry checks, followed by
separately authorized boot, capture, timing and image-quality testing. No such
hardware qualification is claimed here. Z8, Z40 and Hub builders retain their
separate pipelines and admission checks.
